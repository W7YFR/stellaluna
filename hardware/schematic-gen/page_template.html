Stellaluna Schematics

The Stellaluna Keyer: a K3NG keyer (Arduino Mega), its HAT, the front panel (FP) and the latch board (LB), drawn from the netlist checked on 2026-10-10. Designators use your value codes (R1 = 10k, C4 = 3.3µF, …), so a code appears wherever that value is used. J_FP and J_LB are the HAT’s headers to the front panel and the latch board.
