Stellaluna Schematics

The Stellaluna Keyer: a K3NG keyer (Arduino Mega), its HAT, the front panel (FP) and the latch board (LB), drawn from the netlist checked on 2026-10-10. Designators use your value codes (R1 = 10k, C4 = 3.3µF, …), so a code appears wherever that value is used. J_FP and J_LB are the HAT’s headers to the front panel and the latch board.

Proposed changes

  1. AudioESP32 sidetone: add GPIO26 (DAC2) → C5 1µF → R5 2.2k onto the D52 net for an own-keying sidetone from the ESP32, and a sidetone key line (Mega D46 → 10k / 22k divider → ESP32 GPIO21) that tells it when to sound. Firmware setting st.src (Mega / Adapter / Auto).
  2. AudioAmp drives headphones: HP_NODE goes straight to the amp input, the amp output drives the jack’s tip and ring, and the speaker moves to the jack’s switched contact (T′). Makes VBand audio as loud in the headphones as on the speaker; no new parts.
  3. LatchAdd T3 with R1 10k from Mega D44 and R8 1M base to GND, collector on the gate node: the keyer can power the ESP32 on.
  4. KiCad v1Power-path stage on the barrel input, so the barrel supply and the Mega’s USB can never feed each other.
  5. KiCad v1Link dividers 10k / 20k (3.33V) instead of 10k / 22k (3.44V).
Sheet 1

Power

HAT
Show proposed:

The barrel jack feeds one +5V rail through the fuse. Everything runs from that rail except the ESP32, which gets its 5V from the latch.

Sheet 2

ESP32 power latch

LB · HAT
Show proposed:

A P-channel MOSFET switches the ESP32’s 5V. The button turns it on, the ESP32 holds it on through T1, and the same button doubles as the channel button.

Sheet 3

Mega ↔ ESP32 link

HATno changes

Serial link at 38400 baud plus the VBand key line. The Mega’s 5V outputs reach the ESP32 through dividers.

Sheet 4

Audio

HAT · FP
Show proposed:

The Mega sidetone feeds the main volume (headphones and speaker) and the line out. Received VBand audio from the ESP32 joins at the headphone point only.

Sheet 5

Paddle and speed pots

HAT · LBno changes

Paddle on D30 (dit, tip) and D32 (dah, ring). The speed pots are on the latch board; their 10nF caps sit at the Mega pins.

Sheet 6

Button ladder

FPno changes

Eight buttons on one analog pin. The command button sits directly on A1; each one after it is 1k further down the chain.

Sheet 7

LEDs and OLED

FP · HATno changes

Status LEDs driven straight from pins through their resistors, and the I2C OLED.

Sheet 8

TX keying and straight key

HATno changes

The transistor that keys the radio from D49, and the straight key jack. Both are part of the build; they just aren’t wired up yet.
